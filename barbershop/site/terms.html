Terms and conditions

Last updated: September 2026

1. Bookings

Appointments are booked online or by phone. Your booking is confirmed once you see the confirmation screen and receive a booking reference. Each booking is for the service, barber, date and time you chose.

2. Cancellations and changes

  • Please call us at least 24 hours before your appointment to change or cancel.
  • Cancelling with less than 24 hours notice, or missing an appointment, may affect your ability to book online again.

3. Arriving late

Please arrive 5 minutes early. If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten your service or rebook you so we do not delay the next customer.

4. Prices and payment

Prices listed on this website are in US dollars and may change. Payment is made in the shop by cash or card after your service. Package and offer discounts cannot be combined.

5. First-visit offer

The 10% first-visit offer applies to one service for new customers and cannot be used with other promotions.

6. Children

Children under 12 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian for the whole appointment.

7. Health and hygiene

All tools are cleaned and disinfected between customers. Please tell your barber about any skin condition, allergy or injury before your service, as we may need to decline some treatments for safety.

8. Satisfaction

If you are unhappy with your cut, tell us within 7 days and we will fix it free of charge.

9. Liability

We take great care with every customer. To the extent allowed by law, we are not liable for indirect loss, or for damage to personal belongings left in the shop.

10. Privacy and your data

When you book we collect your name, email, phone number and any notes you add. We use them only to manage your appointment and contact you about it. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties. To ask us to delete your details, email us.

11. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
