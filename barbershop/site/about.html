A neighbourhood shop for over 70 years

Gilded Razor & Co. opened in 1952 as a two-chair shop on Anchor Street. The same brass mirrors still hang on our walls.

Today three barbers carry on that tradition: proper training, sharp tools and time for every customer. We believe a good haircut is the simplest way to feel like yourself.
